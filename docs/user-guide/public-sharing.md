# Public Sharing

Share a read-only snapshot of your portfolio's allocation and holdings with anyone, without exposing your account or your money.

## Overview

Public Sharing lets you generate a link to a public page showing your portfolio's composition: sector and geographic breakdown, and a table of your top holdings by weight. Anyone with the link can view it, whether or not they have a Portfolium account.

!!! note "Amounts are never shown"
    The public page only ever shows **percentages**, never currency values, quantities, or cost basis. Someone viewing your shared portfolio can see that Technology is $32\%$ of your holdings, but never how much money that represents.

## Enabling Public Sharing

1. Go to [Portfolios](portfolios.md)
2. Open the portfolio you want to share
3. Find the **Public Sharing** toggle in the portfolio's settings
4. Turn it on

Once enabled, Portfolium generates a unique, unguessable share link for that portfolio. You can open the **Share** option on a portfolio at any time to view or copy this link.

!!! tip "Preview before sharing"
    Use the **Preview public page** option to see exactly what visitors will see before you send the link to anyone.

## What Visitors See

A visitor who opens your public link sees a self-contained page with:

- Your username as the portfolio owner and the portfolio's name
- The number of positions, sectors, and countries represented
- A **sector allocation** breakdown with percentage bars
- A **geographic allocation** breakdown with percentage bars
- A **top holdings** table listing each position's symbol, name, country, sector, industry, and weight in the portfolio

Visitors without a Portfolium account also see prompts to sign up; visitors who are already logged in see a link back to their own dashboard instead.

The public page clearly marks itself as a snapshot, with a visible badge indicating that monetary amounts are hidden.

## Disabling or Revoking Access

Turn the **Public Sharing** toggle off at any time to make the portfolio private again. Once disabled, the previous share link stops working immediately, anyone who still has it will see an error page instead of your portfolio data.

!!! warning "Turning sharing back on generates a fresh state"
    Re-enabling sharing does not necessarily reuse an old link in a predictable way from a visitor's perspective. Treat the current share link as valid only while sharing is turned on, and re-share the link again if you re-enable it later.

## Privacy Controls

Public Sharing is an all-or-nothing toggle per portfolio: it does not currently offer options to selectively hide individual positions or specific data columns. What's shared is fixed to allocation percentages and the top holdings table described above, no cost basis, quantities, or absolute values are ever included, regardless of settings.

If you hold sensitive or highly concentrated positions you'd rather not disclose even by name, keep that portfolio private and consider maintaining a separate portfolio for anything you intend to share publicly.
