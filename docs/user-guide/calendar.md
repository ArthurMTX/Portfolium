# Calendar

Track earnings dates, market closures, and daily performance for your portfolio, all in one timeline.

## Overview

The Calendar page brings together everything that happens on a given day, whether it's a strong trading day, a company reporting earnings, or a stock exchange closing for a holiday.

It works on the currently active portfolio and combines three types of information:

- **Daily performance** — whether your portfolio gained or lost value on a given day
- **Earnings events** — upcoming and past earnings reports for stocks you hold (and optionally your watchlist)
- **Market closures** — holidays and non-trading days for the exchanges relevant to your holdings

!!! note "Stocks only"
    Earnings tracking applies to individual stocks. ETFs, cryptocurrencies, and other fund-style assets don't report earnings the same way, so they don't appear in the earnings calendar.

## Accessing the Calendar

1. Log in to Portfolium
2. Click **Calendar** in the main navigation
3. Make sure the portfolio you want to review is selected as your active portfolio

The Calendar always reflects the currently active portfolio. Switching portfolios elsewhere in the app updates what the Calendar shows.

## Calendar Views

The page offers two views, switchable from the tabs at the top.

### Calendar Overview

A traditional month grid. Each day shows:

- The daily percentage change for your portfolio (past days only)
- A colored indicator for positive or negative performance
- Small markers for earnings events and market holidays
- Ticker symbols for any stock reporting earnings that day

Selecting a day opens a detail panel with:

- The exact gain or loss for that date, in both percentage and currency terms
- Whether the market was closed (and which exchanges were affected)
- Any earnings events scheduled or reported that day

Use the arrows to move between months, or the **Today** button to jump back to the current date.

Below the grid, a small "Calendar observations" summary highlights:

- Your next portfolio catalyst (the soonest upcoming earnings event for something you own)
- Your largest holding that's about to report
- How many watchlist-only earnings are coming up
- The most recently reported earnings event

### Earnings Calendar

A list-based view focused entirely on earnings, grouped into **This week**, **Next week**, **Later this month**, and **Later**.

Each upcoming earnings entry shows:

- The number of days remaining until the report (or "Today" / "Tomorrow")
- The expected EPS (earnings per share), when available
- The expected revenue, when available
- Whether the stock is actually held in your portfolio or only tracked on your [Watchlist](watchlist.md)

A separate "Past Earnings" section lists the most recently reported results, including the actual EPS and the earnings surprise (how far the actual results came in above or below expectations), when that data is available.

!!! tip "Jump to research"
    Every earnings entry links directly to that asset's research page, so you can dig into the company before or after it reports.

## Including Your Watchlist

By default, the Calendar also shows earnings for stocks on your [Watchlist](watchlist.md) that you don't currently own, so you can keep an eye on companies you're considering.

Use the **Watchlist** toggle near the view tabs to show or hide these events. Turning it off leaves only earnings for stocks you actually hold.

## Refreshing Earnings Data

Earnings dates and estimates are kept in a cache so the Calendar loads quickly. If you want the freshest data immediately, click **Refresh Calendar**.

This queues a background refresh and shows live progress while it runs. Once complete, the calendar reloads automatically with updated figures.

!!! tip
    You generally don't need to refresh manually. Earnings data updates automatically in the background, but a manual refresh is useful right after adding a new position you want to track immediately.

## Market Closures

Days where an exchange relevant to your holdings is closed are marked directly on the calendar. If your portfolio spans multiple currencies or markets, Portfolium checks the holiday schedule for each relevant exchange and flags a day as closed if any of them are shut.

A day can be a **full closure** (all relevant exchanges closed) or a **partial closure** (only some of them), which is shown distinctly on the calendar so you know exactly which markets were affected.
