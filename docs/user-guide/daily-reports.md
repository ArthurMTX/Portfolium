# Daily Portfolio Reports

Get a PDF summary of your portfolio emailed to you automatically at the end of each trading day.

## Overview

Daily reports give you an end-of-day snapshot without having to open the app: how your portfolio moved, which holdings led the day, and what happened in your account — delivered straight to your inbox as a PDF.

## What's In a Report

Each report covers:

- **Portfolio summary** — total value, cost basis, unrealized P&L (amount and percentage), number of holdings, dividends received, and fees paid
- **Daily performance heatmap** — a visual grid of your holdings sized by position value and colored by how much each moved that day, so the biggest movers stand out at a glance
- **Holdings table** — every position with quantity, average cost, current price, market value, and both unrealized P&L and the day's change
- **Recent activity** — any buys, sells, dividends, or other transactions recorded that day

## Enabling Daily Reports

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications**
2. Find the **Daily Portfolio Reports** section
3. Turn it on and save

Reports are sent on trading days (Monday through Friday), after the US market closes for the day. No reports go out on weekends or market holidays. Once a report is sent, you'll also get an in-app notification confirming it was delivered and to which email address.

!!! note "Requirements"
    You need at least one portfolio with transaction history to receive a report — an empty account has nothing to summarize.

## Troubleshooting

### Didn't Receive a Report

- Confirm daily reports are enabled in **Settings → Notifications**
- Check your spam or junk folder
- Make sure your portfolio has at least one transaction — reports aren't generated for empty portfolios
- Reports are only sent on trading days; nothing goes out on weekends or market holidays

### Report Looks Incomplete

- Missing prices for some holdings can leave gaps in the heatmap or holdings table — try refreshing prices from the [Dashboard](dashboard.md) first
- Very large portfolios may take slightly longer to generate; if a report seems delayed, it should still arrive later the same evening
